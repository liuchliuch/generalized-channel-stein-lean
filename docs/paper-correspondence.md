## Shared mathematical conventions

- Families consist of actual complex-linear CPTP matrix maps, not Kraus presentation objects. `IsChannel` and `isChannel_iff_kraus` prove the exact channel semantics used to quantify through finite Kraus witnesses
- `compositeBeta` is **infimum over one feasible tester, then supremum over the alternatives**. `ChannelTest` permits arbitrary finite references and mixed joint inputs. `compositeBeta_eq_pure` uses one simultaneous probability-preserving normal form before all channels
- `familyEntropy` is **infimum over alternatives, then input supremum** of genuine support-aware Umegaki entropy. The input-sized pure supremum equals the arbitrary-reference/mixed-input optimization by proved recovery/purification and DPI
- Umegaki has value `+∞` on support failure. Testing exponents first use the extended negative logarithm, with zero beta giving `+∞` at positive lengths. Optimized finiteness and positive beta are proved from the faithful replacer before any real-coordinate rewrite
- Matrix norms are genuine induced Euclidean operator norms. The diamond norm is the **full unhalved completely bounded trace norm**, over all finite references and all complex trace-norm-unit-ball matrices
- Smoothing witnesses are exactly trace-preserving channels. The factor `δ/2` in probability bounds is proved using equal traces, not imposed by redefining the norm
- Choi matrices are input-first and unnormalized. Canonical purification uses the physical input density and the required transpose relation to its reference marginal
- Positive physical dimensions and positive blocklengths are explicit where the source uses density matrices/block families. Natural-index zero conventions do not impose an F0 axiom

## Definitions, remarks, and Example 24

### Definition 1 (`def:axioms`)

[Families.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/Families.lean) defines `AlternativeFamily`, `IsChannel`, `Admissible`, and actual `tensorBlocks`. F1's channel certification, nonemptiness, compactness and real convexity apply only to positive blocks. F2 uses the same genuine coordinate identification for all channels. F3 supplies one fixed faithful output replacer; its positive powers are derived by `replacer_power_mem`. This is a definition plus supporting implementation, not an extra numbered theorem.

### Definition 14 (`def:quantitative-axioms`)

[QuantitativeFamilies.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/QuantitativeFamilies.lean) defines actual `sitePermutation`, `permuteChannel`, `marginalChannel`, `QuantitativeAt` and existential `QuantitativeConditions`. The insertion state is one fixed faithful τ, not every state or necessarily maximally mixed. `QuantitativeAt.iterated_marginal_closed` and [BulkMarginal.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/BulkMarginal.lean) prove the literal repeated insertion/discard action. [SiteGrouping.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/SiteGrouping.lean) and `BulkMarginal.replacedMap_mem` supply arbitrary-subset regrouping. Empty/full support cases do not assume an F0 member.

### Remark 15 (`rem:marginal-scope`)

The scope assertion is verified by actual types: Theorems 2, 3, 11, 12 and Corollary 13 have only `Admissible F`; their endpoints do not take `QuantitativeAt` or `QuantitativeConditions`. F5 is used in the physical weighted-discard/free-multiplier constructions and the later quantitative completion. No separate proposition is needed to turn this dependency observation into a new assumption.

### Example 24 (`sec:nonfaithfulness`)

[CorrelatedExample.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/CorrelatedExample.lean), `Correlated.example_24`, proves the actual full labeled-partition construction, F1–F5, target exclusion from F1, bounded family entropy, beta≥c(1−ε), and canonical rate zero. The source's pure target is included in the stronger distinct-state endpoint.

[CorrelatedPartitionFormula.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/CorrelatedPartitionFormula.lean), `Correlated.generator_block_formula`, proves that the flag generator really is the tensor product over partition blocks of the advertised correlated mixtures. [CorrelatedSegment.lean](../GeneralizedChannelStein/CorrelatedSegment.lean), `partitionHull_one` and `alternativeFamily_one`, prove the exact displayed one-copy segment equality. Both the construction and the displayed one-copy equality have explicit endpoints.

### Remark 31 (scale of completion cost)

The explanatory balance `m` versus `n/√m` is a heuristic, and the source asserts no optimality of 2/3. The formal quantitative scalar modules prove the actual scale and complementary-regime estimates used by Proposition 30. `IsometricCompletionProperty` contains no active-regime restriction. The index does not invent a formal optimality theorem or count a heuristic as a proved additional result.

## Review of assumptions and conclusions

- `Admissible` quantifies over actual finite-dimensional CPTP maps via the proved Kraus equivalence. Compactness and convexity are imposed on their Choi images; these are linear coordinates for the same channel space. The family need not contain product-only channels, and block zero is unused.
- Main asymptotic contracts 2, 3, 11, 12, and 13 contain F1–F3 only. `QuantitativeAt` supplies precisely the additional permutation and fixed faithful input insertion/discard conditions for the quantitative results.
